Engage your employees and improve team building exercises by adding the Guess Word widget to your employee experience platform. Inspired by "Wordle", this widget can be customized to your brand identity, with hand picked words that are relevant for your organization's message and cultural and communications initiatives.

## Widget Overview

Guess Word is a word guessing game that runs inside your LumApps pages. Employees try to guess the hidden word, which brings a light, playful moment to the intranet and gives people a reason to come back.

## Use cases

Use Guess Word when you want a game or gamification element on your employee experience platform, for example:

- **Employee engagement:** a regular game that keeps people visiting the intranet.
- **Team building:** a shared challenge teams can play and talk about together.
- **Communications campaigns:** pick words tied to a launch, a value, an event or a cultural initiative, so playing the game reinforces your message.
- **Branded experience:** match the widget to your brand identity so the game feels part of your platform.
