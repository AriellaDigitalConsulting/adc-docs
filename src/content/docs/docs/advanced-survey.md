## Advanced Survey

## Widget Overview

The Advanced Survey widget is a powerful enhancement over the default LumApps survey feature. While the basic LumApps survey allows for only a single question and limited control, the Advanced Survey widget supports rich and flexible survey experiences. Users can create surveys with multiple questions, various answer types, and advanced result management options. This widget ensures surveys are engaging, reusable, and fully integrated with LumApps' look and feel.

## Widget Interface

The widget interface is a responsive design developed with LumApps Designer best practices in mind. It allows users to:

-   Create surveys with multiple questions, including free text responses and multiple choice formats
-   Import and export survey questions to reuse or replicate a survey from a past campaign
-   Allow designated users (not only editors) to view and export survey results
-   Export responses in various formats: summary in CSV, PDF, or HTML; detailed and anonymized results in CSV
-   Configure visibility of survey results: keep them private or show them to participants

Survey managers can also:

-   Define an expiration date for the survey to automatically close participation
-   Track survey performance from a management dashboard, even after the widget has been removed from the page

## Widget Settings

-   Define who can respond to the survey (audience restrictions)
-   Set the visibility of results (private, or visible to respondents)
-   Configure survey expiration dates
-   Enable or disable export options
-   Assign additional users as "results viewers"

Installation Instructions This widget requires an external service to function. This service can be:

-   Hosted by our team under a standard Data Processor Agreement
-   Deployed and maintained by the customer’s IT team

The external service ensures secure handling of anonymous data and uses the LumApps identity token for all authentication and authorization processes.

## Added Value

-   Richer survey experiences inside LumApps, replacing outdated or external tools
-   Preserves brand consistency across surveys
-   Facilitates data reuse through import/export features
-   Enables performance tracking and data retrieval even after widget removal
-   Complies with enterprise-level data access control and authentication
-   Supports collaborative evaluation by allowing non-editor roles to view/export results
