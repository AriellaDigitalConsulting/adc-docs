## **Widget Overview**

The Spaces & Communities widget by ADC enhances the native LumApps "follow Space / follow Community" functionality. While LumApps normally allows users to follow Spaces or Communities only by clicking on the Follow CTA directly from the respective Space / Community, this widget overcomes that limitation by allowing you, the page admin, to put a “Follow \[Space / Community Name\]” CTA on any page, including the homepage. This ensures a smoother and more flexible experience for users who want to follow social engagement content relevant for them.

## **Widget Interface**

The widget interface is a responsive design developed with LumApps Designer best practices in mind.

-   Each button corresponds to a specific LumApps Space or Community

-   Buttons visually indicate whether the Space or Community is currently being followed or not.

-   When clicked, the Space or Community is followed or unfollowed in real-time.

-   Once a Space or Community is followed, the button state updates to reflect the change, eliminating redundant interactions.


## **Widget Settings**

-   Configure each button to follow a specific Space or Community.

-   Position multiple buttons anywhere within the same page layout.

-   Styling options can be adjusted through LumApps Designer settings to match your site theme.

-   Add a custom label; i.e., Follow Lorem Ipsum


## **Added Value**

-   Bypasses LumApps' native limitation of having to click into a user profile to follow a Space or Community from anywhere on your site

-   Improves user experience by accurately reflecting the followed status.

-   Enables your employees to easily opt in to Space or Community.
