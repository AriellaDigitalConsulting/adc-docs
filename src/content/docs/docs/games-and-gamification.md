Games and gamification give employees a reason to visit your LumApps platform, take part and come back. ADC offers four widgets for this: two ready-to-use games, and two widgets that show a Bunchball gamification program inside LumApps.

## Games

These games work on their own. You don't need a separate gamification product.

| Widget | What employees do | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Guess Word](/docs/guess-word-widget) | Guess a hidden word in a Wordle-style word game, with words chosen by your organization | Everyday engagement, team building, reinforcing a campaign or value |
| [Soccer Predictions](/docs/soccer-predictions) | Predict soccer match results, earn points and compete on a leaderboard | Engagement during tournaments such as the FIFA World Cup, friendly competition across teams |

## Gamification with Bunchball

These widgets need Nitro Studio Bunchball, a gamification solution from BI Worldwide. Employees earn points, levels and badges for actions in LumApps by completing missions set up in Bunchball.

| Widget | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [Bunchball Leaderboard](/docs/bunchball-leaderboard-widget) | One or more leaderboards of employees' points from Bunchball missions |
| [Bunchball Profile](/docs/bunchball-profile-widget) | Each employee's own gamification profile |

## Which one to use

- **You want a game employees can play right away:** Guess Word or Soccer Predictions.
- **A soccer tournament is coming up:** Soccer Predictions.
- **You want to reward platform use with points, missions and badges, and you have Bunchball:** Bunchball Leaderboard and Bunchball Profile.

For other ways to engage employees, such as surveys and posting widgets, see the Engagement section of the menu.
