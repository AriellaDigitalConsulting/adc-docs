## Widget Overview

Ready to celebrate a milestone within your team community, or an org-wide milestone? Give a celebratory look to your page by adding the Confetti widget



![](/images/1983b05552093725.gif)



## Widget Settings

The widget allows you to customize the message, font and font size



![](/images/e9f0a043f09e3e70.png)



Available emitters. Select how you’d like your confetti to emit on the page from the provided options

![](/images/7e851a31c1aacc3a.png)



Confetti colors. Customize the colors of your confetti to your brand
