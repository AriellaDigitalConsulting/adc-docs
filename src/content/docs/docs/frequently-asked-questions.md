## Do your widgets collect any user data?

No. Our widgets do not collect any user data.

## Do your widgets collect any performance stats?

Yes. Many widgets collect specific information which allows us to support and manage the experiences better for our customers. Here is the information that we do collect:

-   OrgID; (This is the Customer # of your LumApps platform)

-   Domain Name;

-   Environment on which LumApps is installed; i.e., sites-us.lumapps.com, sites-eu.lumapps.com, etc

-   Widget ID; (this is the reference ID to our widgets)

-   Time stamp for which a request has been recorded by our server (to help us analyze if there are any down times)

-   License activation flag to determine the customer Widget Extension license status.


## What is your extension widget support model?

Our response time to support inquiries is typically within 1 - 2 business days upon receipt by our Customers and we use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any defects in a timely manner. If a widget's codebase, interface or experience is broken due to a defect or an update in the LumApps Marketplace for which ADC was not notified of in advance; provided that if a commercially reasonable correction is available and documented by LumApps, we will make reasonable effort to make the corrections and updates on our side for submission to the LumApps Marketplace. We also host a widget status page to notify our customers of any outages with a detailed RCA.

## Do I need to sign any T&C in order to receive a trial of a widget?

No. Given that our widgets do not store any user data, we do not require you to sign any Terms & Conditions for a free trial. We leave this process open to the internal processes and requirements of our customers
