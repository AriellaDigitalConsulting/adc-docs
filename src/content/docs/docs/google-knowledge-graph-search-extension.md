The Google Knowledge Graph Search Extension allows any user to search through the Google Knowledge Graph.

If you are entitled to use the Google Cloud Enterprise Knowledge Graph, contact us in order to leverage the power of the Google Knowledge Graph applied to your domain-specific knowledge and obtain insightful search results directly in LumApps.
